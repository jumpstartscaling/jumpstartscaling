Warning: Outdated Metric

Why "ROAS" is Killing Your Scale

Most agencies are obsessed with ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) reported inside Facebook Ads Manager. But since iOS14, Facebook creates data based on "probabilistic modeling" (guessing).

If you optimize for ROAS, you are optimizing for the algorithm's guess, not your bank account. You end up turning off ads that are actually generating profit, and scaling ads that are just retargeting your existing customers.

Our Metric: MER (Marketing Efficiency Ratio)

Total Revenue ÷ Total Ad Spend. This is the only number that matters. It tells us the true health of the ecosystem. We optimize for Contribution Margin Dollars, ensuring every dollar spent actually prints profit.
