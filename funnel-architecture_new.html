Sales Architecture

For DTC Brands & Low-Ticket Offers

The Headless Cart

ECOMMERCE

The Problem: Shopify themes are bloated. They load unused JS, killing your mobile conversion rate.

The Solution: We decouple the frontend using Hydrogen or Next.js. The site loads instantly. We code custom "Sticky Add-to-Carts" and "Slide-Out Drawers" that feel like a native app.

> Implements: Shopify Storefront API
> Feature: Instant Page Transitions
> Result: +30% Mobile CVR

The Tripwire Engine

SLO FUNNEL

The Secret: How to liquidate your ad costs on day one. We build a "2-Step Order Form" funnel.

The Stack: Step 1 captures the lead (email) even if they don't buy. Step 2 offers a low-ticket product ($27-$47). Immediately after, we hit them with a One-Click Upsell (OTO) that adds pure profit without re-entering credit card info.

> Tech: Stripe Elements + Webhooks
> Feature: Cart Abandonment Recovery
> Result: $0 CAC (Cost of Acquisition)

Lead Generation

For Cold Traffic & List Building

The Pre-Sell Editorial

NATIVE ADS

Stop Selling, Start Educating. Cold traffic hates being sold. We build "Listicle" or "Advertorial" pages that look like news sites (CNBC, Healthline style).

These pages warm up the user, handle objections via storytelling, and prime the click. By the time they hit your offer page, they are pre-sold.

> Tech: AMP-Compliant HTML
> Feature: UTM Parameter Passthrough
> Result: 50% Lower CPCs on Meta/TikTok

The Segmentation Loop

ZERO-PARTY DATA

The Diagnostic. Don't just ask for an email. Ask questions. "What is your skin type?" "What is your budget?"

We build custom interactive quizzes that segment users into different buckets. We then use Liquid Logic to show them a personalized product recommendation based on their answers.

> Tech: Conditional Logic Branching
> Integration: Klaviyo Custom Properties
> Result: 2x Higher Email Open Rates

Inbound Calls

For High-Ticket Agencies & Consultants

The VSL Application

HIGH TICKET

The Setter. If you sell anything over $2,000, you need a Video Sales Letter.

We code a "Timed Reveal" funnel. The "Book a Call" button does not exist until the user has watched enough of the video to be qualified. This filters out the tire-kickers and ensures your sales team only talks to informed prospects.

> Tech: Wistia/Vimeo Player API
> Feature: Watch-Time Tracking -> CRM
> Result: 80% Show-Up Rates

The Calendar Direct

RETARGETING

The Low Friction Play. For warm audiences (email list or retargeting), we skip the VSL.

We embed a custom-styled scheduler (Calendly/GoHighLevel) directly onto the page. No redirects. No fluff. Just "Pick a Time." We use webhook automations to send SMS reminders instantly.

> Tech: Embed API + CSS Overrides
> Feature: Instant SMS Confirmation
> Result: 20% Increase in Booking Volume

The Launch Formula

EVENT BASED

The Sideways Letter. Based on Jeff Walker's PLF. We build a 4-part video sequence page.

Video 1 (The Opportunity), Video 2 (The Transformation), Video 3 (The Ownership), Video 4 (The Offer). We use "Unlock Dates" to prevent binging, building massive anticipation before the cart opens.

> Tech: Scheduled Content Locking
> Feature: Waitlist Viral Sharing
> Result: Massive "Day 1" Spikes

The 5-Day Challenge

COMMUNITY

The Relationship Builder. Sell a low-ticket ($17) seat to a live 5-day workshop.

We engineer the "VIP Upgrade" flow. After they buy General Admission, we upsell them to VIP (Zoom Backstage Pass) for $97. This covers your ad spend immediately, while you build a cult-like following.

> Tech: Facebook Group Gatekeeping
> Feature: Gamified Homework Submission
> Result: High Trust, High LTV
